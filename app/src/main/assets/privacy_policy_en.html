Privacy Policy — Orbit VPN

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Orbit VPN is a client application that helps you connect to proxy/VPN endpoints that you configure (share links or imported servers).

What we collect

The app does not operate a backend that collects personal accounts, analytics, or advertising IDs. Configuration you enter (server links, notes, country tags, favorites, theme/language preferences) is stored only on your device.

Network traffic

When you connect, your device traffic is sent through the endpoint you selected. That remote server is controlled by its operator, not by Orbit VPN. Do not use endpoints you do not trust.

Permissions

The VPN permission is required to create a local tunnel on your device. Notification permission may be requested so the connection status can be shown while the tunnel is active.

Logs

Optional diagnostic logs on the device may include hostnames and connection errors. They are not uploaded by the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

For privacy questions about this client build, use the contact channel published with your distribution of Orbit VPN.

Changes

We may update this policy inside the app when the product changes. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
